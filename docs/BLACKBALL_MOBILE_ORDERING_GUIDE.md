# Blackball mobile ordering — demonstration guide

Open the Blackball customer profile. Swipe the featured gallery, or use its arrows,
dots or keyboard left/right keys. Each card selects a real available menu item.
Motion is finite and disabled when reduced motion is requested.

1. Choose Caffe Latte, Biscoff Latte, Spanish Latte or Matcha Oat Latte.
2. Try Large and Cream cap. The total changes and a photographic approximation
   transitions into view. Size/topping choices and surcharges are labeled as fictional
   demo options; the menu's published base price remains unchanged.
3. Add to order. The product flies to the persistent cart. Review the selection,
   choose Cash at counter or a simulated wallet and place the demo order.
4. Open “Try the fulfillment demo.” With cash, review then confirm payment as the
   simulated cashier; start preparing, mark ready and mark served. These controls
   operate the same record as the staff kitchen, one explicit step at a time.
5. For a wallet, first use “I’ve sent the demo payment.” That is a customer claim,
   not verification. Cashier review and confirmation are still separate steps.
6. Reload and reopen Your current order to see the saved completion/receipt.

Original logos, 104 menu entries, pricing, availability, table context, owner Studio,
market entry and other merchants are retained. Existing browser records gain only
missing curated demo option contracts; owner-defined size/top contracts are preserved.
There is no real payment, identity service, backend or merchant affiliation claim.
Photo variants are approximate; hot/cold photos and exact portions are not certified.
Four products are featured; customization demonstration is limited to the four drinks
above. All other items remain browseable/orderable through the complete menu.
